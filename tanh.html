Euclid's Elements have served as a pillar of mathematical thought for millenia. In the 19th century, the discovery and development of non-Euclidean geometry demonstrated that the foundations laid out in the Elements really describe one particular case within a greater hierarchy of geometries.

In this spirit, Hyperbolic Tangent is a celebration of the non-Euclidean world of hyperbolic geometry, and a longstanding obsession made tangible in the form of tactile crochet works and interactive computer graphics.

Inspired by the sublime beauty and structure of the underlying mathematics, the featured Orveyl project is part of an ongoing effort to build a fantasy realm set in hyperbolic space, and explore what it might be like to live there.
